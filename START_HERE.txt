Georgia Girl Journal

Extract ALL files from this ZIP into one folder. Open Open_Georgia_Girl_Journal.html in Chrome or Edge. Keep the Media folder beside it.

The suitcase is the final puzzle. Click the ticket and the Diggy Graves picture for the video popups. Turn the six lock wheels, or type the date. Unlocking reveals seven photos and starts Circus Psycho. Click any photo to enlarge it, then use Previous/Next and Close. The four unused puzzle spreads have been removed completely.
